How a season works

  • A season is 4 rounds. Each round is a Supply Chain Showdown with its own events, the same for everyone in the league.
  • Pick any league code (a teacher can share one with a class). Everyone with the code is in the same league.
  • You get one try per round. Your season score is the total of your rounds.
