How it works

  • You start with $30,000. Sales are $12,000 a month and costs are $10,000.

  • Fires, supplier failures, cyber attacks, recessions and lawsuits hit at random. The right protection turns a disaster into a small bill.
  • If cash goes below zero, you are out. Score is your final cash, or 100 points for each month you survived. Try the Stress Test first.
