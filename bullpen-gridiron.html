How it works

  • Create a game to get a code, or join a friend's game with theirs.
  • Take turns. On your turn, pick an empty square and type a ticker that satisfies both its row and column.
  • Every category is a real, checkable fact — sector, HQ state, Dow 30 membership, Dividend Aristocrat status, or founding era.
  • Get three of your mark in a row — across, down, or diagonal — to win. Full board with no winner is a draw.
  • Each ticker can only be used once across the whole board, by either player.
