How it works

  • You run a small shop. Each month one thing happens and you choose how to respond.
  • Every choice becomes real accounting entries. If cash goes below zero, you are out of business.
  • Survive all 24 months to win. Score is 100 per month survived plus a bonus for cash left.
  • Profit and cash are not the same thing. Watch what is owed to you and what you owe. Why they differ.
