How it works

  • You sell at $20 a unit. Stock costs $10. Rent and wages are $3,500 a month, and each unsold unit costs $1 a month to store.
  • Supplier news shows before you order. Customer demand is revealed after, so you have to guess.
  • Paying next month keeps cash free but the bill is due then. Running out of cash forces a bank loan and a $500 penalty.
  • Score is your net income from the income statement, minus penalties. See how it works in the Statements Lab.
