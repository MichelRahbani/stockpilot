Supply chain, in plain English

Every product you buy travelled through suppliers, factories, warehouses and shops. When that journey goes wrong, the company's numbers show it. Learn how it works, then break a company's supply chain and watch what happens.

Suppliers→Makers→Warehouses→Shops→You

Goods move right. Cash moves left. The timing gap between them is where companies get in trouble.
