How it works

  • You'll see a real price chart for a real S&P 500 company with the most recent day hidden.
  • Guess whether the next trading day closed up (Bull) or down (Bear).
  • Guess right, your streak grows and you move to a new stock. Guess wrong, the streak ends.
  • Every chart is real historical data, pulled fresh each round - not simulated.
